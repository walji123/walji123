## Featured Project: E-Commerce Weekly Revenue Forecasting

- **Problem:** How can an online retailer use past sales to better predict future weekly revenue and plan inventory?
- **Approach:** Analyzed more than 541,000 UK retail transactions, cleaned the data, examined sales patterns, and compared several forecasting models.
- **Results:** A model combining a simple baseline with Random Forest achieved the lowest average weekly error of £55,670, compared with £59,202 for the baseline. The analysis also showed that the model struggled to predict the sharp increase in sales during November.
